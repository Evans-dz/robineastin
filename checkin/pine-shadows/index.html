Pine Shadows

Checking in

Welcome! Here is everything you need to get settled.

Get directions to 3810 Mortensen Ln

  1. Check in from 4:00 pm

    Arrive any time after 4:00 pm. The keypad lets you in, so there is no one to meet and no key to pick up.

  2. Find the cabin

    Pine Shadows is 3810 Mortensen Ln, in the pines on the northeast side of Island Park Reservoir, off US-20 between Ponds Lodge and Mack’s Inn. It is the aqua-roofed cabin beside the game garage.

  3. Park in the circular drive

    There is room for trucks, trailers and RVs. If you booked the trailer hookup, it has water and electric for one camping trailer.

  4. Let yourself in

    Enter your door code on the keypad. It is in the check-in instructions we email you three days before you arrive.

  5. Get on the Wi-Fi

    The network name and password are on the welcome sign and in the welcome binder inside.

  6. Make yourselves at home

    The welcome binder has the house guide, things to do and the checkout steps. Firewood for the fire pit is provided.

Garbage
Pickup for both cabins from the blue PSI garbage dumpster is early Thursday morning. You can leave the garbage can by the cabins and then pull it out to the road for pickup. Please have the garbage out before Thursday morning.
Bears
Bears live around Island Park. On the National Forest, food and trash must be stored in a vehicle, a building or a bear-proof container from March 1 to December 1. Carry bear spray and give moose plenty of room, especially cows with calves.
Fire
Fireworks are banned on the National Forest. Check current fire restrictions before any campfire.
Emergencies
Call 911. Island Park has no hospital or 24-hour emergency room.
