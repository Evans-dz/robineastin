Privacy policy

Last updated [your launch date]

Who we are

Wilderness Escape Rentals, Island Park, Idaho. Questions: info@wildernessescaperentals.com or (208) 351-1033.

What we collect

What you send us. When you call, text or email us, we receive what you include, such as your name, dates and group size.

Bookings. Reservations and payments are handled by our booking system, OwnerRez, which collects the details needed to book and pay. Its own privacy policy applies to that information.

Usage data. This site does not use analytics or advertising cookies. Our host keeps standard technical logs (such as IP address and time of request) to run and protect the site.

Who processes it for us

OwnerRez (bookings and payments), our website host, and our email provider. Fonts are served from this site, not a third party. We don't sell or rent personal information.

How long we keep it, and your choices

We keep booking and inquiry records as long as we need them to host you and keep normal business and tax records. Contact info@wildernessescaperentals.com or (208) 351-1033 to see, correct or delete what we hold about you.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update it here and change the date at the top.
