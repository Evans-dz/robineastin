Pine Shadows

Checking out

Thanks for staying with us. A few quick things before you head out.

  1. Check out by 10:00 am
  2. Leave all the beds unmade
  3. Start a load of towels

    Gather the towels in the laundry room and start a wash. If you have time, move them to the dryer. It helps our cleaner get ready for the next guests.

  4. Run the dishwasher and clear the food

    Load and start the dishwasher; the cleaner will put the dishes away. Take your food out of the fridge, freezer and cupboards.

  5. Take out the garbage

    All garbage goes in the designated can outside. Pickup is on Tuesday.

  6. Lock up

    Shut and lock the windows and doors. Press the lock button on the keypad as you leave.

  7. Sign the guest book

    We love reading about your stay.

Come back in another season

Island Park is a different place in winter, spring and fall. Book your next stay on our website anytime.
