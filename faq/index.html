Questions

Frequently asked questions

What guests usually ask before they book. Still wondering about something? Email us at info@wildernessescaperentals.com.

Fast Wi-Fi, made for working from the cabin

Both cabins have free high-speed Wi-Fi that is great for getting work done. Video calls, sending big files and streaming all run smoothly, so you can take a meeting at the kitchen table in the morning, head out to Yellowstone after lunch, and stream a movie on the smart TV that night.

Booking

How do we book?

Right here on our website. Pick a cabin and your dates on the booking page, see your total, and pay securely online. Your rental agreement follows by email.

Will we see the price before we book?

Yes. Choose your dates in the booking box and it shows the nightly rate, taxes and fees, and your total before you pay.

Can we book both cabins for a reunion?

Yes. Pine Shadows (sleeps 12) and Wilderness Escape Retreat (sleeps 9) are next door to each other with the activity garage between them, so up to 21 people can stay together. Book each cabin for the same dates, or email us and we will help you set it up.

What is the cancellation policy?

Full refund more than 30 days before arrival, half refund more than 14 days before, and no refund within 14 days.

Do you allow pets or smoking?

No pets, and no smoking or vaping inside the cabins. You are welcome to smoke outside. Guests must be 21 or older to rent.

In the cabins

Is the Wi-Fi good enough to work from the cabin?

Yes, it is fast and it is great for work. Both cabins have free high-speed Wi-Fi that handles video calls, big uploads and streaming with no trouble. Answer email on the deck in the morning, spend the afternoon in Yellowstone, and stream a movie on the smart TV at night.

What toiletries are provided?

Shampoo, conditioner, body wash and hand soap are in both cabins, along with all the linens and towels.

What is in the kitchen?

Both kitchens are stocked for real cooking: pots, pans, dishes, coffee and spices, plus a Crockpot, an Instant Pot, an electric grill, a waffle iron, food storage containers and Ziploc bags for packing a park lunch. Pine Shadows also has a dishwasher.

Is there air conditioning?

Yes. Pine Shadows has air conditioning upstairs: a mini split unit on the wall that gives you cool or warm air. Wilderness Escape Retreat has an air conditioner in the master bedroom, plus ceiling and portable fans, and summer nights here are cool, usually in the 40s.

What else is provided?

Firewood for the fire pit, a crib and a highchair at both cabins, and board games and puzzles at Wilderness Escape Retreat. Bring bear spray if you plan to hike.

What is the activity garage?

A garage between the cabins with ping pong, foosball, an electronic basketball game and cornhole, plus a microwave, an extra fridge, a serving table, and extra picnic tables and chairs for big group meals. It can be added to a Pine Shadows stay for $30 a night, up to $150 per stay.

Can we bring a trailer or RV?

Yes. Pine Shadows has a circular drive with room for trailers and RVs, and water and electric hookups for one camping trailer for $75 per stay.

What time are check-in and checkout?

Check-in is from 4:00 pm with a keypad, so you can arrive whenever suits you after that. Checkout is 10:00 am.

Getting here and the area

How far is Yellowstone?

About 30 minutes to the West Entrance at West Yellowstone, roughly 30 miles. In summer, get to the gate early: lines and road work can add time.

Where do we buy groceries?

Sam Patch Co. in Island Park is the closest grocery store, about 15 minutes away. Dave’s Jubilee in Ashton is about 45 minutes and Broulim’s in St. Anthony about an hour. Elk Creek Station, 10 minutes away, has gas and the basics.

Is there anything to do besides Yellowstone?

Yes. The Grizzly and Wolf Discovery Center in West Yellowstone, about 30 minutes away, has grizzly bears and gray wolves you can watch up close. It is open every day until 6:00 pm, so go during the day. In the evening there is live theatre: the Playmill Theatre near Mack’s Inn, the Yellowstone Playhouse dinner theatre above Henrys Lake, and the Westgate Theatre in West Yellowstone. Or stay in for the fire pit and the stars.

Where are the nearest restaurants?

Lakeside Lodge, Pond’s Lodge and Boondocks Roadhouse are about 10 minutes away, and Cafe Sabor and The Parlor at Mack’s Inn about 15. West Yellowstone has dozens more. Our guide lists them all with drive times.
